# Unicorn Auctions

> Unicorn Auctions is a Chicago-based online auction marketplace for rare spirits and
> fine wine. Founded in 2019, it consigns, authenticates, appraises and photographs
> every bottle before listing it in one to three weekly digital auctions, and operates
> a vault storage service for collectors. Bidding happens on the web platform and on
> native iOS and Android apps. **Unicorn Auctions publishes no public API, SDK,
> webhook surface or developer program.** The `api.unicornauctions.com` backend that
> serves its own clients is private and undocumented, and `robots.txt` disallows
> `/api/`. There is nothing here for an agent to call.

## Status for machine consumers

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, gRPC/Protobuf or WSDL contract is published anywhere.
- No MCP server, A2A agent card, or `/.well-known/` discovery document is served on any host (probed 2026-09-02, all 404).
- No first-party SDK exists on npm, PyPI, RubyGems, Maven Central, NuGet, crates.io or pkg.go.dev.
- The GitHub organization exists but publishes zero public repositories.
- Agent access to inventory is therefore limited to the public HTML site, which is crawlable (`robots.txt` allows `/`, disallows `/api/`, `/auth`, `/exchange`) and publishes a sitemap.
